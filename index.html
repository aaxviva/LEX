Alexey Viventsov — Videographer in Israel

Alexey Viventsov is a videographer and director based in Israel, working under the brand Move Creators. He specializes in premium automotive content for car dealerships, and also produces commercials, podcasts, online courses and events using cinema cameras.

He is also the founder of Derector AI, a platform that generates advertising videos for car dealerships.
